Privacy Policy — Allowance Portal

Last updated: October 20, 2026

Allowance Portal is an offline-first allowance-claim calculator for Haldia Dock Complex staff, developed and operated by Anish Mondal (contact: anishmondal5686@gmail.com).

What data the app handles

  • Your Google account email/ID — used solely to keep your synced records isolated under your own storage folder.
  • The claim details you enter: name, designation, pay details, attendance, movement records and allowance computations.

Where it is stored

  • On your device, in app-private storage.
  • If you enable Drive Sync, in your own Google Drive, inside an app-specific folder ("Allowance App"). The developer has no access to your Drive or its contents; the app only touches files it created itself.

Permissions

Google Drive drive.file scope: create/read/modify only the app's own backup files in your Drive. It cannot see any other files in your Drive.

What we don't do

  • No servers, no analytics, no advertising, no tracking.
  • Your data is never sold or shared with anyone.

Deleting your data

Sign out in the app, then delete the app's files from your Drive (or clear the app's data on your device). That removes everything.
